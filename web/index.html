The moment

A joke the system refused to tell.

Humour lowers anxiety — until it makes a promise the system cannot keep. This is the whole governance story in one interaction.

Copy draft

💻 Your computer is getting slow… this year you get it back!

↓

⚔️ Adversarial Reviewer — rejected, High severity

Promises an outcome the system cannot know. “You get it back” implies the full purchase price returns. Alex is anxious about being wrong — an unkept promise is the fastest way to confirm that fear.

↓

🕊️ Brand Guardian — cleared, 4/7 → 7/7 rules passing

Honesty

What this is, and what it is not.

It is

  • A working multi-agent prototype you can run offline
  • Grounded: every sentence traces to a knowledge-base section
  • Deterministic — the demo cannot fail on stage
  • Coloured from the palette taxfix.com actually serves

It is not

  • Tax advice, or an official Taxfix knowledge base
  • Real data — every person, company and amount is invented
  • LLM-backed — the agents retrieve and compose, they do not reason
  • An official brand guideline
